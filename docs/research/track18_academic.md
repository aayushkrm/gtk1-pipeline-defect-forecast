## A. Newest forecasting with honest validation

S1. Comprehensive Empirical Evaluation of Probabilistic CGR Models (2023, IPC-family; abstract via index). 57,678 box-to-box-matched external anomalies with FOUR successive ILIs across 12 pipelines (mixed products/vintages/coatings); historical train-test split — first 3 ILIs predict 4th-ILI depth; statistical + graphical (unity-style) scoring of mean and uncertainty. Link: https://exa.ai/library/publication/jy6d84wbxrj (full text not verified). RELEVANCE: only study at this scale that withholds a full ILI run as test. USE as honesty template (3-ILI fit → next-run predict → report both tails); ADAPT its model roster as P2 baselines. Cites Li/Fredine Enbridge validation line (S12).

S2. IRTH "Analytic Derivation and Validation of a Probabilistic Corrosion Growth Model" (PRCI REX 2025, extends PRCI-331). Closed-form depth distribution from full ILI history with Gaussian depth errors — no Monte Carlo; validated on 1,294 segments / 3+ ILI runs / 4 operators; future-depth vs field-measured UNITY PLOT beats two-point and all-point linear models; two-point shown most deviant. Link: https://irthsolutions.com/blog/probabilistic-corrosion-depth-prci-rex-2025-2 (vendor blog — full text not verified). RELEVANCE: analytic non-negative-growth posterior is the correct likelihood for matched-depth data. USE posterior form; ADAPT "2-point vs full-history uncertainty" figure as our matched-pair diagnostic.

S3. Same group at Pipeline Technology Conference 2024 (abstract). 165k miles ILI + 50k repaired metal-loss anomalies; depth distribution feeds modified-B31G PoE directly. Link: https://www.pipeline-conference.com/abstracts/analytic-derivation-and-validation-probabilistic-corrosion-growth-model (full text not verified). RELEVANCE: scale anchor. ADAPT PoE-coupling note; REJECT as method (same model as S2).

S4. Smith et al. PPIM 2021 → European follow-up (755 onshore pipelines, ~3.5M joints, 2010–2020). JOINT-level DNNs (no defect IDs!) predict log10 anomaly density (RMSE 0.10), log10 corroded area (0.12), max depth (0.35 mm) on a held-out 20% with UNITY PLOTS; ~98% of depth predictions within ±1 mm. Link: full PDF via https://airdrive.eventsair.com/eventsairaueprod/production-conferenceco-public/cf808a8be3e54655a318a72e98182e20 (conference PDF, verified via fetch). RELEVANCE: closest published analogue of ID-less forecasting — aggregation at joint level sidesteps matching entirely. ADAPT joint/weld-anchored aggregation as our fallback representation; USE its unity-plot reporting bar.

S5. Zhang & Yang, GAM-based internal-corrosion growth from mass ILI data (ICBDC 2023). n=1,107 internal defects, covariates = elbow/weld distance, initial depth, pressure, elevation; 98.4% "accuracy" vs BPNN 94.8 / GRNN 95.7, 0.5 s fit; needs NO multi-ILI matching. Cites Zhang et al. 2014 + Dann & Maes 2018. Link: https://doi.org/10.1145/3624288.3624289 (full text not verified). RELEVANCE: additive smooths of weld-distance covariates transfer to our feature set. ADAPT with caution — metric undefined, internal-only, no blind run.

S6. Cui & Wang 2023 (J. Infrastruct. Preserv. Resilience, open access — verified). Multi-year ILI of one steel pipeline: KNN/SVM/RF/LightGBM link adjacency features to severity (RF best); Gumbel max-depth + Weibull number-density growth models. Honesty signal: growth subset keeps ONLY continuously-increasing maxima; mean depth FELL plano-wide from small-new + maintenance features. Link: https://doi.org/10.1186/s43065-023-00081-w. RELEVANCE: documents exactly our threshold-drift confound. USE its filtering audit; REJECT its growth fit for our use (selection-biased by construction).

## B. Matching beyond Dann–Maes

S7. GIE Group matrix probability-matching (industry method page, verified). Weld-anchored alignment, then all-pairs probability matrix over location/class/area-overlap with tool tolerances + global permutation optimum; 50k anomalies in minutes; beats list-loop matching (order-dependent double-matches). Link: https://giegroup.net/novedades/matcheo-de-anomalias-proveniente-de-distintas-corridas-ili-a-partir-de-algoritmo-para-la-prediccion-de-corrosion/?lang=en. RELEVANCE: global-assignment matcher in production. ADAPT as ablation candidate vs greedy/Hungarian/Voronoi (must pass R1–R4 + block-CV per INDEX decision 1).

S8. New Century + TC Energy 2022: auto-correlate ILI features with NDT laser scans, algorithmically aligned, with quantitative UNITY-PLOT scoring; doubles as ILI bias-factor estimator for CGR correction. Link: https://exa.ai/library/publication/yxj7gk4y6qh (full text not verified). RELEVANCE: only automated ILI→field-truth alignment protocol found. USE to specify our dig-sheet alignment fields (request item).

Amaya-Gómez 2016 pattern-recognition paper survives only as a citation in S-cited work (Energies 2023 §2 refs it). ## D. Prevalence-shift-robust evaluation

S9. Beyene–Chen–Kifle 2024 (Biometrical J. 66:2300135): nonparametric TIME-DEPENDENT precision–recall for right-censored data + R package tdPRC (bootstrap SEs, Beran weights). Links: https://doi.org/10.1002/bimj.202300135; https://kmbeyene.r-universe.dev/tdPRC/tdPRC.pdf (both verified). RELEVANCE: newly-reported-defect prediction under threshold drift IS a censored time-to-event problem with shifting prevalence. ADAPT tdPRC + tdROC at fixed horizons per run-pair; never pool pairs (track 4 rule).

S10. Concordance line: Heagerty & Zheng 2005 (incident/dynamic AUC → concordance, via tutorial https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6584037/, verified) + Gerds et al. 2013 IPCW concordance under covariate-dependent censoring (https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.5681) + Blanche/Schoop competing-risk extensions (verified via review). RELEVANCE: IPCW/censoring-weighted concordance is the honest rank metric when verification digs censor the field. ADAPT Uno/Gerds-style weighted c-index for dig-priority ranking; none of these authors apply it to pipelines (gap noted, not a flaw).

## E. Negative results (prized)

S11. ROSEN PPSA 2016 signal-matching + prediction-method selection (verified PDF): unity-plot validation of predicted-vs-actual 3rd ILI; explicit warning "blind reliance on statistics can lead to under-conservative results"; representative-CGR strategy must be matched to pipeline's measured activity. Link: https://www.ppsa-online.com/papers/16-Aberdeen/2016-09-ROSEN-paper.pdf. RELEVANCE: pre-registers our conservatism-first reporting rule. USE as guardrail quote.

S12. Enbridge "Validation of CGR Models" (Li et al. 2017, NACE; 6 pipelines × 3 MFL runs, 700+ field depths, 60k ILI features; feature- vs signal-matching, joint- vs feature-level, PRCI EC-1-2 probabilistic arm). Findings: overestimated CGR → wasted digs, underestimated → failure; no method universally accepted. Cites Nessim IPC2008 (C-FER uncertainties), Al-Amin IPC2012 (hierarchical Bayes), Desjardins/Spencer IPC2010. Link: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390869552_Validation_of_Corrosion_Growth_Rate_Models (full text not verified). RELEVANCE: field-truth validation protocol (predict → 3rd ILI + digs). USE its design; C-FER companion rule: when growth-to-error ratio is small, "uncertainty too large for meaningful inference" (https://www.cfertech.com/insights/obtaining-corrosion-growth-rates-from-repeat-in-line-inspection-runs-and-dealing-with-the-measurement-uncertainties).

Supporting honesty signals (no separate numbers): Energies 2023 §3.3 — 202,378 GWNUM+SIPRD matches collapse to 136,200 after dropping negatives, only 3,241 long-term positives; field ML review npj 2026 (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41529-026-00761-4): "clear gap in validating against noisy real-world ILI data"; TinyTransCorrosion 2026 (R² 0.64, n=215): authors flag single-site, needs external validation. Theory-guided static-dmax cluster (Song npj 2023 AdaBoost+SHAP/ALE; Mesghali 2024 RF MAE 0.588; Miao 2024; all on Velázquez-259 soil set): REJECT as forecasters (cross-sectional, n≈259), USE SHAP/ALE interpretability pattern only.
