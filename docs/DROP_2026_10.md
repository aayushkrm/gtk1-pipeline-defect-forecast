### PK2-2025 depth units

Depth is stored as a value plus a unit: `мм` 3,643 rows, `% от t` 1,820, `% от t эквив.`
173, `% от Dn` 143, empty 344. All corrosion rows carry `% от t`. The recheck takes depth
from `% от t` rows only, so `мм` rows never enter the 10% cut. Confirm this reading with
the partner.

550 m). Inside the 2 m window, match is 0.000, so every future row at 10% or more counts as
new by construction. The 2020 "Номер трубы" numbering does not follow the 2025 tube log.
Partner question.
